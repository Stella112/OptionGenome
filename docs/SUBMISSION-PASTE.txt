=== TITLE ===
OptionGenome

=== SHORT ===
An autonomous options trading desk on Alpaca paper trading where the AI is the least-trusted part. Code builds and checks every trade; the model only ranks a shortlist, and a deterministic Risk Officer can overrule it. 2,057 decisions, 6 trades.

=== LONG ===
Most trading agents ask an LLM what to trade and then do it. The model always answers, and it never says "nothing looks good, don't trade." In trading, not trading is most of the job.

OptionGenome flips that. The model is the least-trusted component in the system.

HOW IT WORKS

MarketDNA classifies the market from arithmetic only: EMA20/50, ADX(14), realized vs implied volatility, IV rank, and an economic-events calendar. It outputs a permission set (which structures are legal, how many lots), never an order. It withholds permission entirely when implied vol is not at least 5% above realized, the one condition where selling premium has no edge.

Roll Desk screens the live SPY option chain (about 2,000 contracts) and builds fully specified defined-risk candidates: put credit spreads and iron condors, 5 and 10 points wide, shorts near 0.16 to 0.28 delta, ranked on credit net of round-trip bid-ask cost per dollar of risk.

Qwen-2.5-72B (via Featherless) picks one and writes one sentence why. That is its entire authority. It cannot change a strike, expiry, size or price. It never sees equity, buying power, the risk budget or the rules. Its reply is parsed as hostile input: shape-checked, ID verified against the shortlist, extra keys ignored. "override_risk_officer": true changes nothing.

The Risk Officer runs 15 deterministic checks and is the only thing that can commit capital. It does not trust the ticket: width, credit, max loss, DTE and every leg relationship are recalculated from OCC symbols and live quotes. All checks run, so a refusal lists every reason. Any exception is a DENY. Limits: max loss 0.75% of equity, 2% new risk per day, 5% drawdown to flatten-only, one lot, three open structures.

Every open position is re-evaluated each pass: take profit at 50% of credit, stop at 2x, mandatory flatten at 1 DTE.

ALPACA

All reads go through the MCP server (72 tools discovered at runtime, none hardcoded). All writes go through the Alpaca CLI (mleg, limit, day). A test walks the source tree and fails the build if anything crosses that boundary.

WHAT RUNNING IT LIVE CAUGHT

Bugs a green test suite missed: capability discovery matched a read tool to an order-placing tool. Credits were sent with the wrong sign, so every fill was worse than the limit. 49ms of clock skew denied every trade. Each is now a named constant with a regression test.

EVIDENCE

Every decision lands in an append-only fsynced journal. The dashboard shows the last decision end to end: what the model chose, in its own words, and what the Risk Officer did about it.

2,057 decisions so far. It refused 2,051 of them. Down $96 on $100k paper, and every dollar is accounted for.

Paper trading only.
